Sharing

Share clips from the Home screen with Share to Discord. Copy to paste works in any chat with no setup. Posting to a channel needs a webhook link, added here. Either way, ReKapture shrinks clips that are too big and converts them so they play right in the chat.

Discord channels

Each channel needs its own webhook link.

  1. In Discord, hover the channel and click the gear to Edit Channel.
  2. Open Integrations, then Webhooks, then New Webhook.
  3. Click Copy Webhook URL and paste it above.

You need the Manage Webhooks permission on that server. Anyone with the link can post in the channel, so keep it to yourself.

Upload limit

Posts follow the server's limit, not your Nitro.

Clips bigger than this are shrunk before posting. Shorter clips keep more quality.

About

Updates

Checking…

Check for updates automatically

Once when ReKapture starts and every few hours. Nothing installs without you clicking Install.

How it works. Windows' own capture API hands each frame to your NVIDIA GPU's dedicated video encoder (NVENC) without it passing through the CPU. That's the same encoder chip the NVIDIA App uses, so the cost to your games is about the same.

Where things are. Settings are saved in your AppData folder. If something goes wrong, the log file says why.

Video encoding by FFmpeg (LGPL). ReKapture is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA. NVIDIA, GeForce and NVENC are trademarks of NVIDIA Corporation.
