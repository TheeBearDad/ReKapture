### New: controller shortcuts
- **Save replays, record, and turn Instant Replay on or off from your controller.** Works with Xbox controllers and PlayStation controllers (DualSense, DualSense Edge, DualShock 4), wired or Bluetooth, while you play.
- **Defaults:** Save replay is **View + Menu** on Xbox and **Create + Options** on PlayStation.
- **Make your own:** Shortcuts & overlay → Controller. Click a shortcut, press the buttons together and let go.
- **Hold for half a second** (optional) avoids accidental saves, since games still see the buttons you press.
- ReKapture only reads your controller; it never changes its lights or settings.

ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Or download **ReKapture-Setup-2.7.0.exe** below.
