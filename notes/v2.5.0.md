The first release on GitHub. From this version on, ReKapture **updates itself**: it checks here when it starts and every few hours, and shows an **Install update** button on the About page when there's something new.

**Download:** `ReKapture-Setup-2.5.0.exe` (installer, recommended) or `ReKapture-Portable-2.5.0.zip` (no install).

### What's in ReKapture
- **Instant Replay** with one hotkey (Alt+Shift+F10). It can run all the time or only while you're playing a game.
- **Follows your game automatically.** Records just the game's window, with a folder per game.
- **Discord on its own audio track**, or left out entirely.
- **Every setting unlocked:** resolution, aspect ratio, up to 240 fps, H.264, HEVC or AV1, and bitrate.
- **Clip editor:** frame-exact trim and per-track volume.
- **Discord sharing:** copy to paste, or post to a channel.

### New in 2.5.0
- Automatic updates from GitHub Releases. Every download is checked against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before it installs.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because ReKapture isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
