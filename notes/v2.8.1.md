### Screenshots, improved
- **New camera icon** when you take a screenshot, with its own color and opacity, next to the Instant Replay and Recording icons (Shortcuts & overlay → Overlay). "Show on screen" previews it.
- **Screenshots now save to Pictures\Screenshots** by default, sorted into a folder per game. Change it under Replay & saving → Screenshots. Screenshots taken with 2.8.0 stay where they are and still show in Recent clips.
- Fixed: a changed screenshot shortcut now works right away, without restarting ReKapture.

ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Or download **ReKapture-Setup-2.8.1.exe** below.
