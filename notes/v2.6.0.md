### New: small icons instead of pop-up notices
- **Replay saved:** a rewind icon appears in the corner of your screen.
- **Recording:** a red dot when it starts, a stop icon when it ends.
- **Your choice of corner, color, opacity and size**, and how long they stay up: 1, 3 or 5 seconds, or a custom time.
- Try them with **Show on screen** under Shortcuts & overlay. Prefer the old text notices? Switch Style to **Text notices**.
- Problems, like a failed save, still show a short text notice so you know what happened.

ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Or download **ReKapture-Setup-2.6.0.exe** below.
