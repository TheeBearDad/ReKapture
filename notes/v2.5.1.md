### Fixed
- **The Instant Replay bar on Home could freeze** and stop filling, for example after moving the window or alt-tabbing. Replay itself kept working; only the display stopped updating. It now recovers by itself within a second.

If you have 2.5.0, ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Otherwise download **ReKapture-Setup-2.5.1.exe** below.
