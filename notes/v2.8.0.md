### New: screenshots
- **Press Alt + Shift + F1** to save a full-resolution screenshot of the monitor your game is on, exactly as you see it: DLSS and other in-game effects, plus overlays from other apps like Steam, Discord and NVIDIA. ReKapture's own icons and the mouse pointer are left out.
- **Works any time**, whether or not Instant Replay is on.
- **PNG or JPG:** PNG is lossless; JPG is about a quarter the size. Choose under Replay & saving → Screenshots.
- **Controller too:** set a button combo under Shortcuts & overlay → Controller → Take a screenshot.
- A small **camera icon** confirms each screenshot (it uses your replay icon's color). Screenshots show up in Recent clips and can be shared to Discord like clips.
- With HDR on in Windows, screenshots are saved in standard (SDR) color.

ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Or download **ReKapture-Setup-2.8.0.exe** below.
