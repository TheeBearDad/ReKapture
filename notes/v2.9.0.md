### New: the quick bar
On-screen buttons to **save a replay, take a screenshot or record** with the mouse. Turn it on under **Shortcuts & overlay → Quick bar**.

- **Invisible until you need it:** by default it only fades in when the mouse comes near its corner, and while it's hidden, clicks go straight through to your game.
- **Left-click** a button to use it. **Right-click** the bar to keep it on screen; right-click again to let it hide.
- **Put it anywhere:** pick a corner, or drag it by the dots. It stays clear of the taskbar.
- **Your look:** opacity with the mouse away (0% = invisible) and on it, three sizes, and your icon colors. A thin line shows how much Instant Replay has ready (red while recording).
- Never in your clips or screenshots, and clicking it doesn't take focus away from your game.
- **Anti-cheat:** the bar never touches games. Some anti-cheats look for windows drawn over games, so by default it disappears completely while Riot Vanguard, Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, FACEIT, EA Javelin, GameGuard, PunkBuster or Anti-Cheat Expert is running. Hotkeys and controller shortcuts keep working.
- It needs a visible mouse pointer, so it suits windowed play, like a capture card or remote play app. Games in exclusive fullscreen can cover it.
