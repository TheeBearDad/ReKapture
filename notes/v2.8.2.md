### Screenshots on the Home page
- **New Screenshots tab** next to Recent clips: your 24 newest screenshots as a grid of pictures. Click one to see it full size, share it to Discord, open it or show it in its folder.
- A green dot on the tab means there's a new screenshot you haven't looked at yet. ReKapture remembers which tab you were on.
- Replays and recordings no longer push screenshots out of the list (and the other way round): each tab keeps its own newest items.
- "Open clips folder" becomes "Open screenshots folder" on the Screenshots tab.
- Thumbnails that fail to load now try again, then show a picture icon instead of an empty box.

ReKapture offers this update on its own (About → Install update). Or download **ReKapture-Setup-2.8.2.exe** below.
